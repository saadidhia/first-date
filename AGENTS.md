# AGENTS.md

First Date على الطريقة التونسية: a two-player date game in Tunisian Arabic (RTL), built with TanStack Start, self-hosted with Docker Compose (Node server via Nitro, Postgres, Caddy).

## Architecture

- `src/routes/index.tsx`: landing page with create/join room forms. The host picks the room mode first (`src/components/mode-picker.tsx`): `date` (First Date, 2 players), `friends` (صحاب, 2 to 7 players), `board` or `board_friends` (ثنية العرس, below), stored in `rooms.mode`.
- `src/routes/room.$code.tsx`: the whole room experience (join gate, chat UI like Messenger, composer with suggestion chips, timer, private result card). A friends room shows as a group chat: a lobby with the host's start button, who already answered, and the group report. In the lobby the host sees an ✕ next to each other player to remove them (`kickPlayer`), only while the room is still waiting. Once a regular question is revealed in a group of 3+, a `RevealCard` shows every option with who picked it (name chips, a bar, 👑 on the majority), replies in their own words, who ran out of time, and a verdict line (everyone agreed, X alone against the group, everyone different, split). Rooms of two keep the chat bubbles. In a `date` room only, soft looping background music plays by default at a low volume (`src/components/room-music.tsx`, the file `public/romantic.mp3`): a header button opens a popover with an on/off toggle and a volume slider (both remembered per browser in `localStorage`: `firstdate:music:on` / `firstdate:music:vol`, default 12%), and it fades out and pauses while anyone is talking in the voice call (`voice.talking`), then fades back in. `ChatRoom` now owns the single `useVoice` instance and passes it to both `VoiceBar` and `RoomMusic`, so there's still one WebRTC connection.
- **Board games**: two more room modes, `board` = ثنية العرس (a couple, 2 players, starts when the partner joins) and `board_friends` = ملك القعدة 👑 (2 to 4 friends, the host starts it from the lobby). ملك القعدة is a friends' day out, not the road to a wedding: `squareFor(i, team)` gives it its own stops (البداية, القهوة, الماتش, البحر, الماكلة, السهرية, and 👑 الكرسي at the end), turns ⚡ into 🎤, and has no عمّتو (those squares become questions or plain); the winner is crowned ملك/ملكة القعدة. A road of 48 squares from التعارف (0) to العرس (47) on an 8×6 board that snakes up from the bottom right. A couple shares one pair of pawns and takes turns rolling, with 20 rolls to reach the wedding (else they lose); friends race with one pawn each and play on until they all reach 👑 الكرسي (`rooms.board.finished` keeps the order of arrival: the first is ملك/ملكة القعدة, then 🥈 🥉). A friend who arrived stops rolling but still votes. Each friend has 20 rolls (`rolled`); anyone still on the road after that is ranked by position, and `result.ranking` lists everyone, first to last. Squares: 💬 question, ⚡ عراك, 🚧 عقبة (a couple gets a different obstacle each time, picked from `OBSTACLES` in `board.ts`, each with its own step back, from −2 to −4 (the owner's choice: −4 for a fight, عمّتو جات, jealousy; −3 for the family obstacles and no house; −2 for بو فلان موش موافق, no money, the wedding postponed); some with one of their names like "بو سارة موش موافق"; it shows on screen as they land, `rooms.board.obstacle`), and milestones (أوّل قهوة, العايلة, الخطوبة, الملاك, الحنّة). A couple both answer: same answer +2 / different −1, and on ⚡ (always a hard question) +1 / −2; their bank is `src/lib/bank/board.ts`. With friends the player who rolled never answers (`answerersOf`): 💬 is "صحابك يحكمو", a question about that player (`{name}`, `{m|f}` for their gender) whose options each carry a move (+2, +1 or −1, and not always kind = +2 / roast = −1: on a third of the questions the roast pays +1, on another third +2, so the points are a surprise), and ⚡ shows as 🎤 تحدّي (`squareFor`), a dare done on the voice call that the others rate 🔥 +2 / 👍 +1 / 👎 −1 (the player who rolled must keep the mic on at least `MIC_SECONDS` (5 s) or gets `MIC_PENALTY` (−2) whatever the votes: on each poll during a dare, `getBoardState` reads their `voice_members` row and `trackMic` adds up the time it's on and unmuted; the dare card shows the counter and a "حلّ المايك" button); every friend's vote counts, the move is the sum of their points (`verdict`), no votes means no move. Only the player who rolled ever sees each option's points, before and after the vote (`getBoardState` sends `moves` to them alone); the voters never do. Once the vote closes everyone sees who voted for what and where the pawn went. Friends bank: `src/lib/bank/board-friends.ts`. Both banks have three levels (easy until square 16, medium until 32, then hard; ids `board_<e|m|h>_<n>`, `fq_<e|m|h>_<n>`, `fd_<n>`, append-only). The rules are pure functions in `src/lib/board.ts` (tested in `board.test.ts`); the whole game is one jsonb `rooms.board`, and `src/lib/board.functions.ts` (`getBoardState`, `rollDice`, `answerBoard`) saves a move only over the `seq` it read, so two players can't both move. After a question closes, nobody can roll for `REVEAL_SECONDS` (8 s, `holdUntil`, checked by the server) so everyone can read the answers; the answers card stays up and the roll button counts down. Timeouts are applied when anyone polls: the dice rolls itself after `ROLL_SECONDS`, a question closes after `ANSWER_SECONDS`. `RoomPage` asks `getRoomPreview` (`board: true`) and shows `src/components/board-room.tsx` instead of the chat; it stages each change (the dice tumbles, pawns hop square by square, then the question sheet or the outcome). The end card has the same "share to your story" button as the report (`src/components/share-result.tsx`, shared with the room page): a couple's picture shows 💍 and their number of turns (or the square they reached), a friend's shows their medal, their place and the ranking (`shareInput`; `makeShareCard` takes `big` and `progress` for the ring). Tapping a played square opens what happened there (`SquareHistory`: the question, who picked what, how far it moved; each option's points only on questions about me). That history is never stored on the server: each browser records the questions as they close, from what it was shown, in `localStorage` (`firstdate:board-history:<code>`). Board rooms have no `questionIds`, no answers rows and no report. On `/admin` they show in the game list with 🎲 (couple) or 👑 (friends), gold links, the turn and each player's square (`board` in `listGames`), and a "يلعبو توّا" section lists every unfinished game with a player online. Each live game row has a "تفرّج على اللعبة" link to `src/routes/admin_.watch.$code.tsx` (`/admin/watch/$code`): a read-only spectator view of one room (chat: revealed answers + the current question and who answered; board: positions, the open question/dare, the log and result), polled from `getAdminRoom` (admin-only). It is a pure read — no lastSeen, no activity, no advance — so watching is invisible to the players and changes nothing.
- Fun layer in the room: **reactions** (😂 😳 ❤️ 🔥 under each revealed regular question, one per player and question, table `reactions`, server fn `react`; the newest question shows all four, older ones only the ones used; someone else's reaction floats up over the chat; they only exist while the game runs: deleted when the room reaches `done` and again in `getMyResult`, and `react` refuses once it's done), a **streak** (`matchStreak`: same answer N times in a row, everyone in a group; 🔥N in the header, confetti + banner from 3), a **halfway card** after question 15 (`HalfwayCard`: % of same answers so far, the closest friend in a group, what's still to come), **game sounds** (`src/lib/sfx.ts`, synthesised with Web Audio, no files; `firstdate:sfx:on`; switch in the music popover for dates, a bell button for friends) and a **share card** on the report (`src/lib/share-card.ts`: a 1080×1920 story PNG with the score only, never answers, sent to the share sheet or downloaded).
- `src/lib/room.functions.ts`: all game server functions (`createRoom`, `joinRoom`, `startRoom`, `kickPlayer`, `spin`, `playJoker`, `react`, `getRoomPreview`, `getRoomState`, `submitAnswer`, `reportScreenshot`, `getMyResult`). `joinRoom` locks the room row to enforce the player limit (`MAX_PLAYERS`). `kickPlayer` lets the host of a friends room remove a player while it's still `waiting` (not the host, not themselves); the removed player's next `getRoomState` poll fails `authorize`, which the client turns into a "you were removed" screen (`removed` in `useRoomState`) and stops polling.
- `src/lib/questions.ts`: the question registry (Tunisian dialect), timings and `pickQuestionIds(mode, seen)`. Every question has exactly 3 options (the server accepts option index 0–2). It holds the hand-written classics (explicit ids) and expands the big themed banks in `src/lib/bank/` (compact format, see `bank/types.ts`; ids are `<category>_<position>`, so those files are append-only). 1000 questions in total (with marriage, votes and spins); the newest ones at the end of each bank are the bolder (still respectful) ones.
- Each room gets 30 questions. A date: 25 spread evenly over its core topics (money, sharing, family, cleanliness, cheating, freedom, fights, psych), then 5 random marriage questions shown in gold. Friends: 20 over their core topics (how you see your friend, situations, embarrassing, money, secrets, fights), then 8 votes and 2 private questions (`bank/friends-3.ts`, ids `vote_<n>` / `spin_<n>`, read with `roundFor(id)`; they have no options and no scores). On a vote everyone picks one of the others (stored in `answers.vote_for`); the counts only show in the report, never who voted for who. Before the first private question the host turns the wheel once (or it turns by itself after `SPIN_SECONDS`) and the server picks a random player; that same player gets both private questions (`rooms.spins`, question index → player id) and is the only one who answers, in free text; the answers only show in the report. Once per game they can play the joker (`playJoker`): the question is swapped for another `spin_` one and the timer restarts; `rooms.joker` keeps the index and the skipped question, shown crossed out in the report. A friends room of 2 can't vote, so `startRoom` swaps its votes for regular questions (`withoutVotes`). Older friends rooms ended with 5 `saraha` questions instead; the report still handles them. The browser keeps the ids it has played (`firstdate:seen` in localStorage) and sends them on create, so the next room picks unseen questions first.
- Questions are worded per player by `personalize()` in `questions.ts` (used by `questionFor(id, gender, partnerGender)`): `{شريكك}`/`{صاحبك}` follow the other player's gender, `{masc|fem}` is a word about the other player and `{@masc|fem}` a word about the player answering. In a friends group of 3 or more, `othersGender` returns `'group'`: `{صاحبك}` becomes صحابك and every friends template has a third, plural form `{masc|fem|plural}` (e.g. `{عندو|عندها|عندهم}`, or `{صاحبك|صاحبتك|واحد من صحابك}` when the question is about one person). A test in `group.test.ts` fails if a friends question misses its plural; new friends questions must include it. Options are first-person replies that must make sense alone (the chosen one is shown to the other player), so `submitAnswer` words them for the partner's gender before saving.
- `src/lib/compat.ts`: compatibility scoring and the private note (score, partner portrait, tips, per-topic matches, finale answers), with mode-specific wording. Used for 2-player rooms.
- `src/lib/group.ts`: the report for a friends room of 3 or more (`computeGroupReport`): my score with each friend, every pair ranked, one title per member, questions everyone agreed on, where I was alone, who picked the same answer as who, and everyone's finale answers. `computeRounds` gives the vote counts and spin answers, added to both report kinds.
- `src/lib/marriage.ts`: the final marriage report. Every date ends with 5 of the `MARRIAGE_QUESTIONS` (in `questions.ts` + `bank/marriage.ts`), worded differently for men and women; options are ordered partnership (0) → traditional (2) so both genders compare on the same scale. Partner answers to these stay hidden in the chat and only show in the report.
- Admin pages share `src/components/admin-shell.tsx` (login gate, tabs, logout). `src/routes/admin.tsx` (`/admin`, where you land after login) is the stats dashboard (players, active now, last hour, games per type (First Date, صحاب, ثنية العرس, ملك القعدة: cards, the stacked daily bars, their filter and the daily table; `gamesByMode` and `dailyStats` count each mode on its own), the average match % of finished games (couples; every pair of friends) with how many fall in each band, a daily bar chart with period / type / players-or-games filters over the last 90 days, a daily table, the games someone was in during the last hour, newest game first so rows don't move on refresh). Every game row shows its match % (`matchOf` in `admin.functions.ts`, the same `computeCompat` score players see; a group shows its average and closest pair) once the players answered 3 questions together, and each player chip a mic: green when it's on (pulsing while they talk), red crossed out when they muted it, grey crossed out when they're not in the call (`useAdminVoice` polls `getAdminVoice` every 2 s); `src/routes/admin_.players.tsx` (`/admin/players`) lists who played with who: each game shows its two players linked by a pink (couple) or blue (friends) line (`src/components/admin-games.tsx`, data from `getAdminGames`), with a type filter and name search; `src/routes/admin_.funnel.tsx` (`/admin/funnel`) is the completion funnel (created → partner joined → played → reached the report) and the traffic sources, over a 7/30/90-day window (`getAdminFunnel`); the source is guessed by `trafficSource(referrer, userAgent)` in `src/lib/traffic.ts` and stored on `rooms.source` when the room is created (`createRoom` takes the page `ref`). `src/routes/admin_.messages.tsx` (`/admin/messages`) is the secret-message inbox, fed by `getAdminStats` in `src/lib/admin.functions.ts`. Protected by a signed session cookie (`src/lib/admin-session.ts`): login checks `ADMIN_EMAIL` / `ADMIN_PASSWORD` from env, the cookie is signed with `SESSION_SECRET`, and admin-only server functions call `getAdminEmail()`. There is no signup.
- Secret messages: `src/routes/sir.tsx` is a public page (`/sir`) where anyone can send the admins a message. Messages are anonymous: the form doesn't ask for an Instagram username (a link can't detect the account). The server only records which app the link was opened in (`source`, guessed from the in-app browser user agent, e.g. Instagram). Old messages may still have an `instagram` name. Stored in `secret_messages`. Server functions are in `src/lib/secret.functions.ts`: sending is public, with a global cap of 20 messages per minute and a honeypot; reading, marking as read and deleting are admin-only. The admin dashboard has a "الرسائل السرّية" section with the link (copy / share) and the inbox.
- Players pick a gender (`players.gender`, 'male' | 'female', nullable for old rows) before creating or joining a room.
- Voice chat: everyone in a room is in a group call (`src/components/voice-bar.tsx`, under the room header) from the moment they open it, waiting phase included, while the game runs and for 5 minutes after it ends (`rooms.ended_at`), then it closes for everyone. Players join as listeners (no mic, no permission asked); the bar has a single mic button (red = muted, nobody hears; green = everyone hears), the first tap asks for the mic. Every connection keeps an audio slot (transceiver) so the mic is put in with `replaceTrack`, without reconnecting. A reopened page sends a `hello` signal so the other side starts a new connection right away, and signals sent before the page joined are skipped. Audio goes straight between the players (WebRTC, every member connected to every other one; the smaller player id makes the offer). `src/lib/use-voice.ts` runs it in the browser; the server only relays setup messages through `voiceSync` in `src/lib/voice.functions.ts` (tables `voice_members`, who is in the call / muted / speaking, and `voice_signals`). Who is talking is measured on the audio itself in the call, and read from the server by players who didn't join. STUN only by default; set `TURN_URLS` / `TURN_SECRET` (the optional `turn` compose profile runs coturn) for networks that block direct audio.
- Ads and content pages (AdSense policy: no ads on screens without publisher content). `__root.tsx` only has the `google-adsense-account` meta tag (site verification); the ad library is loaded by `<AdSenseScript />` (`src/components/adsense.tsx`) and only on reading pages: home, `/guides`, `/guides/$slug`, `/about` (the last three use `ContentShell` in `src/components/content-shell.tsx`). Never add it to room, AI, admin, `/sir` or error screens. Room and AI pages are `noindex`; `public/robots.txt` blocks them and points to `/sitemap.xml` (`src/routes/sitemap[.]xml.ts`, built from `GUIDES`). Articles are data in `src/lib/guides.ts` (slugs are public URLs: don't rename live ones; newest first).
- `src/lib/use-screenshot-guard.ts`: a best-effort screenshot deterrent and blur overlay.
- `src/lib/session.ts`: stores the per-room player token in localStorage.
- `db/schema.ts`: Drizzle schema (rooms, players, answers, events, reactions, voice_members, voice_signals). `authorize(code, token)` lives in `src/lib/room-auth.ts`, server only. Migrations live in `db/migrations` (applied by `db/migrate.ts`, run by the `migrate` compose service) and are generated with `npx drizzle-kit generate --name <name>`. Never edit applied migrations.

## Game flow (server-driven)

`rooms.phase` moves waiting → intro → question → reveal → … → done; the first spin question goes spin (waiting for the host) → spinning (the wheel animation, chosen player known) → question → reveal, the second one straight to question. A date leaves `waiting` as soon as the guest joins; a friends room stays there (others can still join) until the host calls `startRoom`, and can't be joined after. Nothing runs on a schedule. `getRoomState` (polled by both clients) calls `advance()`, which moves the room on once `phaseEndsAt` has passed. Every transition is a conditional UPDATE (matching on id, phase and current_index), so two clients polling at once can't double-advance. `submitAnswer` jumps straight to `reveal` when every player in the room has answered (on a spin, as soon as the chosen player has).

## Conventions / decisions

- UI wording: plain Tunisian, no unclear French/English loanwords. A room is called "لعبة" (not روم), the result is "التقرير السرّي" (not نوت), screenshots are "تصوير الشاشة" (not capture).

- Players are authorised by a random UUID token. Every server function checks it against the room code.
- The other player's answer text is never sent to the client before its question is revealed.
- The result is computed for the requesting player only, so each note stays private.
- Notes are phrased around "شخصية" (feminine noun) so they read naturally for any gender. Tunisian 2nd person ("إنتي", "تحب") is gender-neutral.
- Theme: a dark Sidi Bou Said night palette in `src/styles.css` (Tunisian red primary, `--gold`, `--sidi-blue`), with `--love-gradient` (red to gold), `.bg-love`, `.text-love`, `.btn-love` (gradient button with glow), `.surface` (raised card), `.eyebrow` (small gold label above section titles), `.bubble-mine`, `.zellige` and `.hero-shade`. The landing hero uses `public/wedding-hero.webp`; the flag is `src/components/tunisia-flag.tsx`. The `<html>` element is `dir="rtl"`, so use logical utilities (`ms-`, `me-`, `start-`, `end-`, `rounded-es-` …).
